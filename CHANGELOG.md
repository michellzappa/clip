## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-14

- First release. Clip keeps a clipboard history in the menu bar.
- ⇧⌘V opens a picker of recent text clips. Type to filter, and ⏎ pastes
  into the app you were in. The picker shows line and character counts.
- The menu bar item lists the last ten clips.
- Settings has General, History and About pages on the shared house window.
- Launch at login and the Accessibility permission row come from HouseKit.
